Catarina Lucas Herrera

Full-Stack Developer · Human-Computer Interaction

Catarina Lucas Herrera is a fourth-year joint-degree student at the University of Chicago, working toward a Bachelor of Science and Master of Science in Computer Science. While she focuses on full-stack development, object-oriented programming, and system design, she is curious to try everything in tech — from wearable technology to sustainability and computing. She has a talent for easily placing herself in a different perspective, which has been immensely useful when designing for a wide range of communities, whether designing for a child, an incarcerated individual, or an individual with lupus. Catarina is more than ready to transition the knowledge gained through the rigorous UChicago curriculum into industry, and is always up for a challenge.

Involvement & Interests

Around UChicago's campus, she debates global policy with Chicago Model United Nations (CHOMUN) and connects with fellow engineers through the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE). She stays rooted in community through the Organization of Latin American Students (OLAS) and MEChA, two spaces that keep her close to home while she's away at school. She also serves as a Resident Assistant (RA) with Housing and Residence Life since 2025 — a role she sees as keeping her just as invested in people as she is in building things for them. When she's away from a keyboard, Catarina is usually planning an outdoor adventure (at the study abroad offices), baking some intricate cake (piping skills are unmatched), painting nature or wildlife (feathers and leaves have such refreshing brushstrokes), or biking around Hyde Park (she's biked from the South Side to the North Side of Chicago multiple times).
